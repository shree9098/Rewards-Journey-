Consumer Electronics — E-commerce Market Snapshot

Market: United States
Category: Consumer electronics
Estimated customer spending in 2026: $565 billion
Expected growth from 2025: 3.7%
Research checked: August 17, 2026

What this means for an online seller

U.S. customers are expected to spend about $565 billion on consumer technology products and services during 2026. The market is expected to grow by 3.7% compared with 2025, showing continued customer demand.

Common products in this category include:
- Smartphones and accessories
- Laptops and computer accessories
- Headphones, speakers, and other audio products
- TVs and displays
- Smart-home devices
- Smartwatches and fitness wearables
- Gaming products

Important note

An individual SKU does not have a “market cap.” In e-commerce, useful SKU-level measurements are:
- Selling price
- Units sold
- Sales revenue
- Conversion rate
- Profit margin
- Market share

Simple example

If an electronics SKU sells 1,000 units at $50 each, its gross sales revenue is $50,000. This is the type of number an e-commerce seller should compare with the larger category opportunity.
